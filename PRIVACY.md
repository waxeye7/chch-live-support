# Privacy Policy — chch live

Effective: 1 October 2026

chch live ("the app") is a free companion to [chch.live](https://www.chch.live). This policy covers what data the app touches.

## What the app does NOT do

- No account or sign-in.
- No advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
- Nothing is sold or shared for marketing. The app is free with no in-app purchases.

## Location

If you allow it, the app reads your device's location **once per use of the Nearby tab** to fetch reports near you. Your coordinates are sent as query parameters to `api.chch.live` (the public data API the app is built on). The location is not stored by the app, not linked to an identity, and not shared anywhere else. Deny permission and the app falls back to Cathedral Square, Christchurch.

## Third-party data

All content — reports, buses, arrivals, headlines, images — is served by chch.live and its upstream public sources (Metro / ECan realtime GTFS, CC BY 4.0). Report photos load through chch.live's image proxy. Those services see normal request metadata (IP address) like any website visit; their own policies apply.
